Privacy Policy for RealSarthi CRM

Effective date: 25 July 2026
Application: RealSarthi CRM (com.dealflow.app)

This Privacy Policy describes how personal and business information is handled when you use the RealSarthi CRM mobile application (the “App”). RealSarthi CRM is a real-estate CRM and sales-operations tool for agencies, owners, and staff.

The App is operated by the provider of RealSarthi CRM (the “Operator”). Where an agency (“Agency”) uses the App for its business, the Agency typically decides what customer and staff records to enter; the Operator provides the App and supporting cloud services so the Agency can operate its CRM.

1. Information you and your agency provide

Depending on your role and features used, the App may process:

Account and authentication information

  • Email address and password (or other credentials used to sign in)
  • Authentication identifiers from sign-in providers used by the App (Firebase Authentication and/or Supabase Auth)
  • Role and agency/profile context needed to authorize access (for example owner, staff, or platform staff roles)

Agency and staff information

  • Agency/business profile details entered in settings (such as agency name, office address, support contacts, and related business identifiers the Agency chooses to store)
  • Staff roster details (such as name, phone, email, employee identifiers, role, branch/area, and permissions)
  • Optional profile or branding images uploaded by users

Leads and client (CRM) information

  • Names, phone numbers, alternate phone / WhatsApp numbers, and related client profile fields
  • Requirements and preferences (for example purpose, property type, areas, budget ranges, notes, source, assignment, and timeline/activity records)

Property, deal, rental, and business records

  • Property/inventory listings and related details, including property-owner contact details when entered
  • Deal and payment/brokerage records, notes, and related financial fields entered by the Agency
  • Rental / agreement records (including tenant and landlord contact details when entered)
  • Expense and similar operational records when those features are used
  • Tasks, follow-ups, site-visit records, and in-app notification/activity records

Uploaded files and media

  • Photos and images (for example property media, site-visit photos, profile or company images) when you capture or select them
  • Documents or files the Agency uploads through document/import features when used

You (or your Agency) choose what to enter. The App is not a consumer property marketplace; it stores business CRM data your team provides.

2. Information collected when you use certain features

Location (optional)

  • If you grant location permission and use site-visit GPS check-in, the App may record approximate or precise location coordinates associated with that check-in.
  • Location is requested for site-visit check-in. Attendance GPS/selfie verification is not an active production feature of the App.

Camera and photos (optional)

  • If you grant camera or photo-library permission, you may upload images for properties, site visits, profiles, or related CRM use.

Device and diagnostic information

  • App version and basic device information (such as brand, model, and OS) may be included when error or diagnostic events are recorded to help operate and fix the App.
  • Network connectivity state may be used so the App can synchronize data when online.

Local device storage

  • The App stores data on the device (including caches, drafts, sync queues, and session information) so features can work offline and then synchronize when a connection is available.

Biometric / app lock (optional)

  • If you enable biometric or PIN app lock, the App uses the device’s local authentication features to unlock the App. Biometric templates remain under the control of the device platform; the App uses them to gate local access.

Communications handoff (optional)

  • The App may open the device phone, SMS, or WhatsApp apps with a number you select. Those apps are separate services with their own terms and privacy practices.

3. Features and services not claimed as active collection

The following are not described here as active production collection by the App at the time of this policy:

  • Mobile push notification delivery via Expo Notifications / FCM (push registration is not enabled in production App code)
  • Firebase Crashlytics or Firebase Analytics SDKs (not integrated as production SDKs in the App)
  • Device contacts address-book access
  • Operator-operated SMS gateway sending
  • Attendance GPS pin or selfie verification as live production capture

In-app CRM “notifications” may still exist as records inside the product without using mobile push delivery.

4. How information is used

Information is used to:

  • Provide CRM features (leads, inventory, deals, rentals, staff coordination, network/agency tools, tasks/follow-ups, and related workflows)
  • Authenticate users and enforce role- and agency-based access
  • Synchronize offline changes to the Agency’s cloud backend when online
  • Store media and documents the Agency uploads for business operations
  • Record optional site-visit check-in location when that feature is used
  • Diagnose errors and keep the App reliable
  • Respond to support requests you send to the Operator or that your Agency administrator escalates

5. Service providers and sharing

The App relies on infrastructure providers to operate. Confirmed providers used by the App include:

  • Supabase — backend database, authentication bridge/session support, and file storage used for CRM sync and uploads
  • Firebase (Google) — authentication (and limited Firebase backend usage where configured)

These providers process data on behalf of operating the App. Agencies may also share CRM data internally with their own staff users according to roles and assignments configured in the App.

The Operator does not sell your personal information. Information may be disclosed if required by law, to protect rights and security, or with your (or your Agency’s) appropriate instruction.

Third-party apps you open from the App (phone, SMS, WhatsApp, maps, and similar) are governed by those providers’ policies.

6. Security

The Operator and service providers apply access controls and transport security appropriate to the services used (including HTTPS for network communication with configured backends). Role-based access and agency scoping are used in the product to limit what staff can see.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Do not treat any CRM app as completely secure. Use strong credentials, limit staff permissions, and follow your Agency’s data-handling practices.

7. Retention and deletion

CRM records are retained while your Agency account remains active and as needed to provide the service, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations. Agencies control much of the business data they enter and can edit or remove records inside the App according to available product features and permissions.

Account / data deletion requests: A dedicated in-app self-serve account deletion flow is not provided in the current App release. To request deletion of an account or associated personal data held by the Operator for RealSarthi CRM, contact support@realsarthi.com (also used as the App’s support contact) and include enough detail to identify the account and Agency. Agency administrators should also be involved when the request concerns Agency-controlled CRM records (leads, deals, staff rosters, and similar), because those records are primarily Agency business data.

Logout ends the local session; it is not the same as deleting an account.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law and your relationship to the Agency, you may be able to:

  • Access or correct information through the App (where your role allows)
  • Ask your Agency administrator to correct or remove Agency-controlled CRM records
  • Contact support@realsarthi.com regarding Operator-held account data or privacy questions
  • Disable optional permissions (location, camera, photos) in device settings (related features may stop working)
  • Sign out of the App

If you are a staff user, your Agency administrator is often the primary contact for CRM record changes.

9. Children

RealSarthi CRM is a business application for real-estate agencies and their staff. It is not directed at children under 13, and the Operator does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 for the App.

10. International processing

Service providers may process data in regions where they operate. By using the App, Agencies and users acknowledge that CRM and account data may be processed in those locations as needed to provide the service.

11. Changes to this policy

The Operator may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Effective date” above will change when material updates are published. Continued use of the App after an update means you should review the revised policy. The public URL for this policy (when published) will be the copy linked from the Google Play listing.
