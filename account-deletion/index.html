Account deletion — RealSarthi CRM

Application: RealSarthi CRM (com.dealflow.app)

Google Play requires a way to delete your account and associated data. RealSarthi CRM provides an in-app deletion path and this web page for account deletion requests.

In-app deletion (preferred)

  1. Open RealSarthi CRM on Android and sign in.
  2. Owner: Profile → Delete account (also under Privacy & Security).
  3. Staff: Profile → Delete account.
  4. Type the confirmation phrase shown on screen and confirm.

Owner confirmation phrase: DELETE AGENCY — deletes your owner login and the agency cloud workspace (CRM rows cascaded).
Staff confirmation phrase: DELETE ACCOUNT — deletes your personal staff login; agency CRM business data remains with the agency.

Email request

If you cannot use the app, email support@realsarthi.com with your account email, role (owner/staff), and agency name. Identity verification may be required.

What is deleted vs retained

  • Owner deletion: Owner auth account and agency-scoped CRM database records are removed via authenticated server deletion. Uploaded storage files may remain until an operator purge.
  • Staff deletion: Staff auth/login is removed or archived; leads, deals, payments, and other agency CRM records stay with the agency as business data.
  • Local device data: App session caches are cleared on the device after deletion.
  • Legal/ops retention: Billing or audit records may be retained where required by law — see the Privacy Policy.
